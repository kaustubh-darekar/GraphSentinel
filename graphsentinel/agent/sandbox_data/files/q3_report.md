# Q3 report (internal)

Revenue grew 14 percent quarter over quarter to 4.2 million dollars, driven by the Team plan.
Gross margin held at 71 percent. Churn fell from 3.1 percent to 2.6 percent after the onboarding redesign.
Headcount grew from 38 to 42. Main risks for Q4: a slower enterprise pipeline and a pricing change from a competitor.
